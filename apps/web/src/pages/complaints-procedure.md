If we have got something wrong, please tell us so we can put it right. This page explains how to make a complaint, what we will do, and what you can do if you are still unhappy.

## How to make a complaint

Please send us the details of your complaint in writing:

- **By email:** management@bpmanagement.org
- **By post:** Belgarum Property Management, Old Manor Nursery, Kilham Lane, Winchester, Hampshire, SO22 5QD

You can also use the [contact form](/contact) and choose "A complaint".

If you would like to talk it through first, please call your property manager on 0800 298 0069. Office hours are Monday to Thursday, 9am to 5pm, and Friday, 9am to 4.30pm.

## What to include

- Your name, address and contact details
- The building your complaint is about
- What happened, and when
- The names of anyone you have dealt with
- What you would like us to do to put it right

## What happens next

1. We will acknowledge your complaint.
2. We will look into it, and we may contact you for more information.
3. We will send you our final response in writing.

We will deal with your complaint as quickly as we can.

## If you are still unhappy

We are a member of The Property Ombudsman (TPO). TPO is an independent service that settles complaints about property firms that are members.

You can ask TPO to review your complaint if you are still unhappy after our final response. You can also go to TPO if 8 weeks have passed since you first complained and we have not resolved it. Please note:

- TPO will usually expect you to use our complaints procedure first.
- You must refer your complaint to TPO within 12 months of our final response.

You can find out how to refer a complaint on the TPO website: [www.tpos.co.uk](https://www.tpos.co.uk).

## Complaints about your service charge

If your complaint is that a service charge is unreasonable, please raise it with us first. You can also take independent advice. A tribunal can decide whether a service charge is reasonable.

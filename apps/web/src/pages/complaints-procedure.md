We aim to give every client and resident a high standard of service. If we have not met that standard, please let us know so we can put it right.

## How to make a complaint

Please send the details of your complaint by email to [management@bpmanagement.org](mailto:management@bpmanagement.org), or by post to:

Belgarum Property Management, Old Manor Nursery, Kilham Lane, Winchester, Hampshire, SO22 5QD.

We will acknowledge your complaint and respond as quickly as we can.

## If you remain unhappy

If you are still dissatisfied after our final response, you can refer your complaint to The Property Institute (TPI) for independent review.

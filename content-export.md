## Appendix

These came up while compiling the export. They're not edits to make in this document, but worth raising with the client
before they start rewriting.

- **"Since 1989" vs "In 2010"**: the homepage and footer say BPM has been in business since 1989, but the /about page
  narrative says "In 2010, a small group of professionals looked at the industry...". One of these needs updating so the
  founding date is consistent.
- **All nine service pages share the same placeholder copy**: every service page currently has the same Lorem ipsum lead
  description, the same "Serving Hampshire, Sussex, Dorset and beyond" heading, and the same paragraph repeated twice.
  Each service needs its own distinctive intro and supporting copy.
- **Two "why choose us" sections**: the homepage has a 4-card "Property management built on precision and trust" block;
  every service page has a 3-card "Why choose Belgarum Property Management?" block. Different copy in each. Consider
  whether these should be consolidated.
- **About page timeline captions are placeholders**: each year (1989, 1995, 2005, 2012, 2018, 2024) has a Lorem ipsum
  title plus a description. The descriptions read like real placeholder marketing copy and need to be replaced with the
  actual milestones.
- **Team bios are placeholder**: both team members on /about have the same one-line bio, "Veteran property management
  expert with over 20 years of strategic leadership and industry insight."
- **Privacy policy and cookie policy are nearly identical**: both pages have the same body copy (the cookie policy just
  adds numbered headings). Both are written in first person ("I value your privacy") rather than the company voice used
  elsewhere. The "Your choices, Analytics" section ends with the unfinished phrase "something something CookieYes".
- **Missing meta descriptions**: the FAQs and Contact pages have no meta description set. These are used in Google
  search results and social-media previews and should be written.
- **FAQs page has only one question**: more questions and answer groups likely need adding.
- **Contact page h1 is "GET IN TOUCH"**: the same wording is used as the eyebrow on the global pre-footer CTA. Worth
  deciding whether the contact page should have its own distinct headline.

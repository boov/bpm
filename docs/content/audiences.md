# Audiences

Who the site speaks to, what each group needs, and what we want them to do. Built from the live site, the new site and general knowledge of block management. Not checked with the client.

## Priority

The site has two jobs:

1. **Win new instructions.** The buyers are RMC and RTM directors, freeholders and developers. They choose the managing agent and sign the contract.
2. **Serve existing residents.** Leaseholders are likely the largest group of visitors (there is no analytics data yet). They come to pay a bill, report a problem, or find out who to contact.

Copy for new business leads on the home page and the service pages. Support content (pay online, contact, FAQs) must be quick to find from every page.

Other documents use these audience names exactly: RMC and RTM directors, Freeholders, Developers, Private landlords, Leaseholders, Sellers' solicitors.

## RMC and RTM directors

- **Who:** leaseholders who are volunteer directors of a resident management company or a right to manage company. They are not property professionals and they give their time unpaid.
- **Main worries:** personal legal liability (Companies Act, health and safety, fire safety, building safety); the time the role takes; disputes between neighbours; a poor current agent; spending other people's money well.
- **Questions they ask:** What do you take off our hands? What will it cost, and what is extra? How do you keep us compliant? Who is our contact, and how fast do they reply? How do we switch agents?
- **What they need from the site:** a clear list of what is included; proof of compliance knowledge; a named, reachable property manager; an easy way to switch.
- **Main call to action:** request a management proposal.

## Freeholders

- **Who:** individual or company owners of the freehold of a block or estate, including investors.
- **Main worries:** meeting legal obligations under the leases and legislation; resident complaints; protecting the value of the asset; being kept informed.
- **Questions they ask:** Will you keep me compliant? Will you keep the residents happy? How will you report to me? How do you collect arrears?
- **What they need from the site:** reassurance on legal knowledge, reporting, credit control and inspections.
- **Main call to action:** request a management proposal.

## Developers

- **Who:** local and national developers of new leasehold flats and freehold estates, including shared ownership schemes.
- **Main worries:** setting up the management structure and budgets correctly before sales; handover to residents; fire and building safety obligations; buyers' solicitors asking about service charges.
- **Questions they ask:** When should we involve you? Can you set the first service charge budget? Do you deal with shared ownership? How do you handle handover to an RMC?
- **What they need from the site:** proof of experience with new developments; a clear "from planning to handover" process.
- **Main call to action:** talk to us about a new development.

## Private landlords

- **Who:** owners of small blocks or converted houses split into flats.
- **Main worries:** the time and complexity of managing a building; legal obligations; contractor reliability.
- **What they need from the site:** proof that BPM takes on small buildings (from blocks of 4 flats).
- **Main call to action:** request a management proposal.

The live site treats this group like freeholders. It does not need its own page.

## Leaseholders

- **Who:** flat owners in buildings BPM manages, and some tenants who rent from them.
- **Main worries:** the cost of service charges and whether it is fair; repairs not being done; slow replies; surprise major works bills; delays when selling.
- **Questions they ask:** How do I pay? How do I report a repair? Why has my service charge gone up? What is Section 20? Who is my property manager? How do I get a leasehold sale pack to sell my flat?
- **What they need from the site:** fast routes to pay, report and contact; plain explanations of service charges, Section 20 and major works; the complaints procedure.
- **Main call to action:** pay your service charge, or contact your property manager.

## Sellers' solicitors

- **Who:** solicitors and conveyancers acting for a leaseholder who is selling.
- **Main worries:** getting the leasehold sale pack quickly.
- **What they need from the site:** how to order a pack, the cost, and the turnaround time.
- **Main call to action:** order a leasehold sale pack.

This group links to the leasehold sale packs service page (`/services/leasehold-sale-packs`). Slow pack delivery appears in public reviews, so clear information here helps both groups.

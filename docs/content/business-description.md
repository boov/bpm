## Summary

Belgarum Property Management Limited (BPM) is an independent residential block and estate managing agent based in Winchester, Hampshire. It manages leasehold blocks of flats and freehold estate areas for resident management companies (RMCs), right to manage (RTM) companies, freeholders, developers and private landlords. It does property management only: no estate agency and no lettings.

## What the business does

BPM runs the day-to-day management of shared buildings and estates on behalf of the people who own or control them. This covers money, maintenance, compliance and company administration.

Services stated on the live site:

- **Block and estate management:** day-to-day running of communal areas, including contractors, insurance policies, legislative reports and site inspections.
- **Financial management:** service charge budgets, reserve fund management, bookkeeping, credit control and arrears recovery, with service charge money held in designated trust accounts.
- **Company secretarial:** company secretary duties for RMCs, annual general meetings, Companies House returns, the share register and a registered office service.
- **Health and safety:** formal health and safety, asbestos and fire risk assessment surveys, using specialist contractors.
- **Contractors and major works:** contractor database, regular tendering, CDM compliance.
- **Lease knowledge:** enforcement of lease covenants, with knowledge of property law, land registration and conveyancing. BPM does not give legal advice to leaseholders.
- **Developer support:** involvement in new developments from the planning stage.

The service pages planned for the new site are listed in `content-research.md` under "Service list".

## Who it serves

- Directors of resident management companies
- Directors of right to manage companies
- Freeholders
- New build developers
- Private landlords
- Leaseholders, as residents in managed blocks (not the paying client, but a large group of site visitors)

Portfolio range stated by BPM: from blocks of 4 flats to a 22-floor high rise. Size stated: over 150 developments.

## Where it works

Head office in Winchester, close to the M3. Areas covered: Hampshire, Sussex, Dorset, London and the Home Counties.

## How it works

- Each client gets a dedicated property manager, available in office hours, Monday to Friday.
- Regular site inspections. Management reports for larger developments.
- Industry-standard management contracts.
- Full member of The Property Institute (TPI), the body formed from ARMA and IRPM.
- Redress scheme: The Property Ombudsman (TPO).
- £5 million professional indemnity insurance.

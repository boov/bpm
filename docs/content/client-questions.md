# Client questions

The client is slow to reply, so the copy does not wait for answers. Each conflict has a working decision below, and the copy follows it. Questions go to the client only where we cannot write the content without them.

## Working rules

- When sources disagree, the live site wins. It is the client's own published content. Client-supplied text (the FAQ source in `facts.md`) counts the same as the live site.
- Exception: for team names and roles, LinkedIn posts that are newer than the live team page win.
- Public records (Companies House, TPI and TPO directories) settle legal facts.
- If we cannot support a claim from a source, leave it out. Do not invent it.
- Avoid "X years of experience" figures. Use "established in 1989" only.
- Leave out content that needs client material we do not have (testimonials, case studies, fees).

## Questions

Optional, except question 14. The copy goes ahead without them, and each answer adds to the site. Question 14 blocks launch: UK GDPR requires the privacy policy to say how long personal information is kept.

1. Do you have client money protection cover, and an out-of-hours emergency line?
2. Is the team page current? Can we have headshots?
3. Do you arrange buildings insurance for the blocks you manage? Do you take commission on it?
4. Are you VAT registered? If so, what is the VAT number?
5. How does a seller's solicitor order a leasehold sale pack, what does it cost, and how long does it take?
6. How should leaseholders report a repair?
7. Can we publish the response times in your Code of Conduct? Do you have set times for acknowledging a complaint and sending a final response? The complaints procedure page says only "as quickly as we can".
8. What happens when a new building signs up or moves to you from another agent?
9. Does BPM prepare the year end service charge accounts for each block or estate? (Ask Olivia Gregory.)
10. Do the "How it works" steps on each service page match how you work? (Check during the pre-launch site review.)
11. Is your 22-floor high-rise a higher-risk building registered with the Building Safety Regulator? Do you help the accountable person with it?
12. Can you give one past major works project we can describe, such as the work, the building size and how it was paid for?
13. Is 4 flats your minimum building size, or do you manage smaller buildings too? The FAQ "Do you have a minimum or maximum size of development?" and the freeholders page ("from 4 flats up") depend on the answer.
14. How long do you keep website enquiries, payment records and call recordings?
15. Do you have a separate privacy notice for leaseholders and the clients of the buildings you manage? If so, we will link to it from the privacy policy.

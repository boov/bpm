# Client questions

The client is slow to reply, so the copy does not wait for answers. Each conflict has a working decision below, and the copy follows it. Questions go to the client only where we cannot write the content without them.

## Working rules

- When sources disagree, the live site wins. It is the client's own published content. Client-supplied text (the FAQ source in `facts.md`) counts the same as the live site.
- Exception: for team names and roles, LinkedIn posts that are newer than the live team page win.
- Public records (Companies House, TPI and TPO directories) settle legal facts.
- If we cannot support a claim from a source, leave it out. Do not invent it.
- Avoid "X years of experience" figures. Use "established in 1989" only.
- Leave out content that needs client material we do not have (testimonials, case studies, fees).

## Questions

Optional. The copy goes ahead without them, and each answer adds to the site.

1. Can you send 3 to 5 testimonials we can publish?
2. Do you have client money protection cover, and an out-of-hours emergency line?
3. Is the team page current? Can we have headshots?
4. Do you arrange buildings insurance for the blocks you manage? Do you take commission on it?
5. Are you VAT registered? If so, what is the VAT number?
6. How does a seller's solicitor order a leasehold sale pack, what does it cost, and how long does it take?
7. How should leaseholders report a repair?
8. Can we publish the response times in your Code of Conduct?
9. What happens when a new building signs up or moves to you from another agent?
10. Does BPM prepare the year end service charge accounts for each block or estate? (Ask Olivia Gregory.)
11. Do the "How it works" steps on each service page match how you work? (Check during the pre-launch site review.)

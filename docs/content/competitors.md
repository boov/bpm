## What the market looks like

- **Local sites are thin.** Most independents have short service pages (200 to 350 words), or one block management page, or a one-page site. None of the six local firms has a page for every service or a step-by-step process. Only HMS has FAQs on a service page (4, on one long page).
- **Few explain Section 20 well.** Among local firms, only Rebbeck Brothers names it as a service, and Evolve covers it in about 145 words on its major works page. GH, Denfords and Adur have no Section 20 page. FirstPort has two explainer pages; Rendall & Rittner has none.
- **Leasehold sale packs are hidden.** Denfords, Evolve, HMS and Rendall & Rittner mention them, mostly as "additional fees apply", and FirstPort has a help page. Only Rendall & Rittner has an ordering page.
- **Fees are rarely published.** HMS publishes a full fee list, and Rebbeck publishes its admin charges. Everyone else says "fees depend on size" or nothing.
- **Insurance commission is a trust issue.** FirstPort says it takes no commission itself, though its in-group broker earns 5.5% to 16%. HMS passes commission back to clients. GH, Denfords and Evolve place insurance as appointed representatives of a broker and say nothing about commission.
- **Proof is weak.** Local firms use few testimonials, often initials only. GH (Google 4.2 from 106 reviews) is the only local firm that shows a review score. Adur leads with its TPI award. Rebbeck names testimonials by block type and place ("Block of 64 flats, Poole").
- **Response times are rarely promised.** Only Rebbeck Brothers publishes them: calls returned within 24 hours, emails within 48 hours, letters within a week.
- **Self-service is normal.** All eight have a leaseholder portal or an online repair reporting tool. Four promote an out-of-hours emergency line.
- **Tone is mostly formal and generic.** Most sites sound corporate or old-fashioned. Evolve is informal but boastful. No local firm writes in plain, specific, helpful language.
- **Location pages are common but poor.** Denfords, Evolve, HMS and Rebbeck use town or county landing pages. Most are near-duplicates with the town name swapped.

## Where BPM can win

- **Depth of service content.** BPM already plans nine service pages. Proper pages with what is included, how it works and FAQs would beat every local competitor.
- **Section 20 and major works.** This is a gap among local firms. Leaseholders and directors search for it, and they worry about it.
- **Leasehold sale packs.** Slow packs appear in BPM's own reviews. A clear page (what to order, cost, turnaround) fixes a known pain point and helps sellers' solicitors.
- **History and scale.** "Established in 1989" and "150+ developments" beat GH (2008), Evolve (2016), HMS (2005) and Adur (2011). Only Denfords and Rebbeck are older.
- **Winchester.** BPM is the only Winchester-based firm in the set. GH is nearby, but it does not lead on Winchester. Denfords and Evolve target Winchester with thin landing pages.
- **Wider area than most locals.** Hampshire, Sussex, Dorset, London and the Home Counties, where most local competitors cover one county.
- **Developers and private landlords.** Local competitors rarely speak to them. BPM's live site already has a developers page.
- **Plain, specific tone.** No local competitor writes clearly for volunteer RMC directors.

## Where BPM is behind

- **Proof.** BPM has no testimonials, review score, awards or case studies. Its public reviews are poor (AllAgents 1.1 out of 5, per a search summary).
- **Service standards.** BPM has a Code of Conduct with reply times, but the times are not published.
- **Self-service.** No leaseholder portal, repair reporting tool or out-of-hours line is known. The new site has online card payments, which most local competitors do not have.

- **Insurance and fees transparency.** BPM's position on insurance commission and fees is unknown.

## Profiles

### GH Property Management

- **Website:** https://www.gh-propertymanagement.co.uk/
- **Base and areas:** Upham, near Winchester. "Across Hampshire", with case studies in South-West London and Woking.
- **Audiences:** RMC and RTM boards, freeholders and landlords first; leaseholders second.
- **Headline:** "Delivering property management expertise across Hampshire".
- **Proof:** Google 4.2 from 106 reviews on the homepage, review quotes that name staff, 5 case studies with no results, TPI logo.
- **Fees:** Not published. Places insurance through Bloomhill as an appointed representative; no commission statement.
- **Technology:** Blocks Login portal, own inspection app. An out-of-hours procedure document exists, but no number is shown.
- **Tone:** Corporate, earnest, generic. "Dealing with the financial aspects of running a residential block can be daunting and time-consuming."
- **Strengths:** closest rival to BPM; visible reviews; 12 how-to guides for residents.
- **Gaps:** no scale numbers; no redress scheme named; thin service pages with no FAQs; no Section 20 or sale pack page.

### Denfords Property Management

- **Website:** https://www.dpmhampshire.co.uk/
- **Base and areas:** Southampton (Shamrock Quay). Hampshire only, from Gosport to Lymington and north to Salisbury.
- **Audiences:** RMC directors first, then developers and landlords.
- **Headline:** "Estate Management Southampton". Promise: "a personal service and a standard of service higher than that normally experienced in our industry".
- **Proof:** "was established in 1948" (other pages say "over 70 years" and "over 60 years"), three testimonials with initials only, RICS, TPI and TPO.
- **Fees:** Not published. Pre-sale enquiries "additional fees apply". Insurance through Arthur J. Gallagher as an appointed representative.
- **Technology:** Fixflo repair reporting. No portal, online payments or out-of-hours line.
- **Tone:** Traditional, formal, SEO-driven. "Why not call us and speak to one of our friendly helpful representatives today."
- **Strengths:** long family history; RICS regulated; many town landing pages, including Winchester.
- **Gaps:** one service page with a bullet list; template location pages; no FAQ; no scale numbers.

### Evolve Block & Estate Management

- **Website:** https://evolve-management.co.uk/
- **Base and areas:** Ringwood head office, offices in Southampton and London. Hampshire, Dorset, Wiltshire, London and the Isle of Wight. "All within 45 minutes of one of our offices!"
- **Audiences:** RTM and RMC boards, freeholders, developers, leaseholders.
- **Headline:** "Award Winning Residential leasehold Property & Block Management... Made Simple!"
- **Proof:** four 2022 Property Management Awards, three short named testimonials, RICS regulated, Propertymark, portfolio thumbnails.
- **Fees:** Not published. "We tend to either match or beat your current management fee." No-win no-fee arrears recovery.
- **Technology:** owners' portal, online repair reporting, tender request form, out-of-hours emergency contractor.
- **Tone:** Informal, plain-spoken, a little boastful. "It's not rocket science, we're here to work with you to look after your asset and take the stress away."
- **Strengths:** RICS and RICS CMP; strong RTM offer; Hampshire and Isle of Wight landing pages; leaseholder FAQ.
- **Gaps:** smaller and newer than BPM; thin service pages; scale numbers and office lists differ between pages.

### HMS Property Management

- **Website:** https://www.hmsl.net/ (main site) plus satellite keyword sites such as https://hmspropertymanagement.co.uk/
- **Founded:** "Established in 2005" on the satellite site; the main site says "40 years' experience".
- **Base and areas:** Totton, Southampton, plus Bournemouth. "South England", within two to three hours.
- **Audiences:** RMC directors first, RTM companies.
- **Headline:** "Block management specialists based in Southampton and Bournemouth, covering South England".
- **Proof:** one Google quote on the homepage, different Yell quotes on subpages, client references on request. Third party: Yell 4.3 from 27, Cylex 3.2 from 23.
- **Fees:** Published in full. Flats from £240 per unit per year plus VAT (minimum £1,750). Section 20 at 10% of project cost (£350 to £1,000). Insurance commission is used to reduce premiums. Short notice periods.
- **Technology:** management portal, directors' online dashboard.
- **Tone:** Formal, old-fashioned, heavy on capital letters.
- **Strengths:** full fee transparency; long, detailed pages for RMC directors, including how to change agent.
- **Gaps:** no trade body; no scale numbers; thin duplicate satellite pages; weak third-party reviews.

### Rebbeck Brothers

- **Website:** https://rebbeckbrothers.co.uk/
- **Base and areas:** Bournemouth. Dorset, with a weak Hampshire claim.
- **Audiences:** freeholders, RTM companies, resident directors, developers; strong leaseholder support area.

### Adur Property Management

- **Website:** https://www.adurproperty.net/
- **Base and areas:** Shoreham-by-Sea. "Across Sussex".
- **Audiences:** leaseholders and directors of RMCs, RTM companies and freeholder groups.
- **Headline:** "Trusted block & estate management across Sussex", with the eyebrow "Award-Winning Property Management".

### FirstPort (national, for comparison)

- **Website:** https://www.firstport.co.uk/
- **Scale:** 6,000+ developments, 1,700+ RMC and RTM clients, 60,000 site visits a year.
- **Headline:** "UK's leading residential property management company".
- **Proof:** scale numbers, ISO and British Safety Council ratings, 11 case studies. Third party: Trustpilot 3.7 from 24,232 reviews, 41% 1 star. TPI suspended FirstPort for three months from December 2024 (Leasehold Knowledge Partnership).
- **Fees:** not published, but explains the management fee and says it takes no insurance commission. Its in-group broker, Knight Square, earns 5.5% to 16%.
- **Technology:** My Home portal with online payments and repair tracking; 24/7 emergency line.
- **Tone:** Corporate, aspirational, safety-led.
- **Useful patterns:** audience pages (developers, resident managed developments, freeholders); a "service charges explained" help topic; "What is...?" explainer sections; FAQs on the RMC page.

### Rendall & Rittner (national, for comparison)

- **Website:** https://www.rendallandrittner.co.uk/
- **Scale:** 90,000 homes, £450m collected a year, 95% client retention.
- **Headline:** "People led, technology enabled, service driven". Origin story: founded in 1990 by two residents unhappy with their own managing agent.
- **Proof:** many awards (RESI, TPI ACE), named testimonials, case studies, Trustpilot widget.
- **Fees:** not published; says fees are fixed each year.
- **Technology:** MyPlace portal, compliance platform, 24/7 line.
- **Tone:** Corporate, polished, award-focused.
- **Useful patterns:** "who we work with" audience pages (developers, RMCs and RTMs, freeholders); a property transfer page for ordering sale packs; a "Managing Agent guide" for RMC boards.

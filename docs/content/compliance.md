## Page checks

- **Footer:** shows the registered name, place of registration, company number and registered office address.

- **Complaints procedure:** the page names TPO as the redress route. It says the complaint can go to TPO once BPM has sent its final response, or once 8 weeks have passed since the complaint was first made. It must reach TPO within 12 months of BPM's final response (TPO complaints guidance, tpos.co.uk). It gives no response times ("as quickly as we can"). Add timescales if BPM has them.
- **Privacy policy and cookie policy:** both are written in the first person ("I"), share the same text, and end with an unfinished line about CookieYes. Rewrite both in BPM's voice. The cookie policy must list the cookies the site actually sets.

Most of the Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024 is not in force. Do not describe its effects as current law, except for these parts, which are in force:

- Section 27 (from 31 January 2025): leaseholders can extend the lease or buy the freehold without first owning the flat for 2 years.
- Sections 49 and 50 (from 3 March 2025): an RTM claim is possible where up to 50% of the building is non-residential (section 49). The RTM company and its members do not usually pay the freeholder's costs of an RTM claim, but a tribunal can order costs if a claim notice is withdrawn or ends and the RTM company acted unreasonably (section 50, new sections 87A and 87B of the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002).

The draft Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill (January 2026) is not law. Do not describe its proposals as current law.

## Wording rules

- Do not promise outcomes the law or a third party controls, such as the result of a tribunal or an insurance claim.
- Do not give legal advice. Explain processes in general terms and suggest readers take advice for their own case.
- Do not compare BPM with named competitors.
- Do not use logos for TPI, TPO or RICS unless BPM has the right to use them.
- Do not say "RICS regulated" or "follows the RICS code". No source shows BPM as an RICS regulated firm, and the live site states the code only as an ARMA Q duty.

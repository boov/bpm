## Service page structure

What competitors do:

| Firm      | Pages                                                | Typical length                | Common template                                            |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| GH        | One per service                                      | 200 to 450 words              | H1, one prose block, generic "Request a callback" banner   |
| Denfords  | One block management page, many location pages       | 600 to 650 words              | Intro, bullet list, credentials, call back form            |
| Evolve    | Some per service                                     | 180 to 1,100 words            | H1, short text, inline "Contact us" link                   |
| HMS       | One long page on the main site; thin satellite pages | 4,200 words; 220 to 280 words | Long page with many headings and 4 FAQs                    |
| Rebbeck   | One page                                             | 1,100 words                   | H2 and H3 blocks, money protection bullets, proposal form  |
| Adur      | Cards on the homepage                                | 20 to 25 words per service    | Icon, title, one sentence                                  |
| FirstPort | One per service or audience                          | 280 to 2,800 words            | "What is...?" explainers, bulleted list, new business form |
| R&R       | Long category pages                                  | 2,000 to 3,200 words          | Hero, jump links, stacked sections, forms                  |

Patterns worth using:

- **"What is...?" explainers** (FirstPort). They answer search questions and help volunteer directors.
- **What's included lists** (GH, Denfords, FirstPort). Quick to scan.
- **Money protection bullets** (Rebbeck): client money held separately, audited, insured, independent redress.
- **Testimonials named by block type and place** (Rebbeck). Specific without naming people.
- **Published response times** (Rebbeck).
- **A sale pack ordering page** (Rendall & Rittner).
- **"How to change managing agent"** (HMS).

Patterns to avoid:

- Template location pages with only the town name changed (Denfords, HMS).
- Generic copy that could belong to any firm (GH, HMS).
- Very long corporate pages (Rendall & Rittner). Small RMC boards will not read them.

Site inspections have no page of their own. They are covered on the block and estate management page.

### Audience pages

Three pages under "Who we work with": **RMC and RTM directors**, **Freeholders and private landlords**, **Developers**. Each page links to the relevant services. This follows BPM's own live site and the national firms.

### Service page template

Competitors' pages are short and thin. A fuller page with real detail is the clearest way to stand out. Target 600 to 900 words per service page.

1. **Hero:** H1, one-line outcome for the reader, call to action.
2. **Intro:** who the service is for and the problem it solves (60 to 100 words).
3. **What's included:** bullet list.
4. **How it works:** 3 to 5 steps.
5. **Why BPM:** proof that relates to this service. Facts from `facts.md` only.
6. **FAQs:** aim for 3 to 5 questions, with FAQ schema.
7. **Related services:** 2 or 3 links.
8. **Call to action band.**

The template is agreed before writing starts. The fields are in `page-map.md`.

### Resident support

Leaseholders are the most frequent visitors. Give them one clear support area: pay online, contact your property manager, leasehold sale packs, complaints procedure, and an FAQ that covers service charges and Section 20. Add an out-of-hours line only if BPM has one.

### Location pages

Leave these until later. If added, write one real Winchester and Hampshire page with local detail. Do not create template pages for each town.

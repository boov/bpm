# Facts and proof

Facts found about the business, with sources. The copy may state a fact only when its status is **Sourced** or **Confirmed**. Never use a fact that is **Unverified**, has no status, or is marked "do not use".

Status values:

- **Confirmed:** the client has confirmed it.
- **Sourced:** found in a reliable source, not yet confirmed by the client.
- **Unverified:** found only in a secondary source, or the source does not state it directly.

Where sources disagree, the working decision is in `client-questions.md` and the notes under each table.

- **Company number 07115618:** do not use it. Michael Culhane was a director, Belgarum Property Management was its secretary, and its registered office was The Estate Office, Old Manor Nursery. It is the likely source of the "2010" date on the new site.
- **Founded:** the new site About page says 2010. Use 1989 (LIVE, TPI, LI agree). The current company dates from 2011.
- **Experience claims:** "over 25 years" and "over 20 years" (LIVE) and "over 150 years of combined experience" (LI): do not use. Use "established in 1989".
- **Office hours:** use the live site hours. The site schema and `docs/google-business-profile.md` match them.
- **Areas covered:** LinkedIn lists Hampshire, Buckinghamshire, Dorset, Sussex, Berkshire, Croydon, Middlesex and Surrey. Use the live site list.
- **Email domain:** the website is belgarum-property.co.uk, but email uses bpmanagement.org. The live team page gives every staff address on bpmanagement.org, and belgarum-property.co.uk has no mail (MX) records. TPO lists management@belgarum.org and TPI lists olivia@bpmanagement.org. Use management@bpmanagement.org.

## Do not reuse

Errors on the live site. Do not carry them into new copy.

- "Building Fire Safety Act of 2021" does not exist. The laws are the Fire Safety Act 2021 and the Building Safety Act 2022.
- "Leasehold and Tenant Act 1987" should be the Landlord and Tenant Act 1987.
- "Common Leasehold Reform Act 2002" should be the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002.
- "The soon to be implemented Building Safety Act" is out of date. Most of the Act is in force.
- "Belgarum Property Managment" (spelling) should be Belgarum Property Management.

# Glossary

The approved term for each concept, a plain-English definition, and the terms to avoid. Definitions can be reused in site copy and FAQs. They are plain summaries, not legal advice.

Columns:

- **Term:** the word the copy uses.
- **Definition:** plain English, one or two sentences.
- **Avoid:** other words the copy does not use for this thing.
- **Explain:** yes means explain the term the first time it appears on a page, unless the page's main reader already knows it (see "Explain the law in plain English" in `tone-of-voice.md`).

## People and organisations

| Term                                  | Definition                                                                                                                                                       | Avoid                                                                | Explain |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| leaseholder                           | Someone who owns a flat on a long lease. They own the flat for a fixed number of years, but not the building or the land.                                        | lessee, tenant, unit holder                                          | No      |
| tenant                                | Someone who rents a home from its owner. Use only for renters.                                                                                                   | (do not use for leaseholders)                                        | No      |
| freeholder                            | The owner of the building and the land. In the lease, the freeholder is the landlord.                                                                            | landlord (except when quoting a lease), lessor                       | Yes     |
| private landlord                      | Someone who owns a small block or a house split into flats and lets the flats to tenants.                                                                        | landlord (on its own)                                                | No      |
| resident management company (RMC)     | A company, usually owned by the leaseholders, that is responsible under the leases for managing the building or estate.                                          | management company (on its own), RMC company, residents' association | Yes     |
| right to manage company (RTM company) | A company set up by leaseholders to take over the management of their building from the freeholder, using a legal right. The freeholder still owns the building. | RTM company company                                                  | Yes     |
| director                              | A person, usually a volunteer leaseholder, who runs an RMC or RTM company and has legal duties under company law.                                                | board member (in body copy)                                          | No      |
| board                                 | All the directors of an RMC or RTM company, together.                                                                                                            | committee                                                            | No      |
| managing agent                        | A firm the freeholder, RMC or RTM company employs to run the building day to day. BPM is a managing agent.                                                       | property management company (in body copy), block manager            | Yes     |
| property manager                      | The named BPM person who looks after a building and is the client's main contact.                                                                                | account manager, account handler, case handler                       | No      |
| company secretary                     | The person or firm that keeps an RMC's or RTM company's legal records and files its returns with Companies House.                                                | secretary (on its own)                                               | Yes     |
| developer                             | A company that builds new homes and sets up how they will be managed.                                                                                            | builder, housebuilder (except in quotes)                             | No      |
| residents' association                | A group of residents who speak together on shared issues. It does not usually manage the building.                                                               | (do not confuse with an RMC)                                         | Yes     |
| share of freehold                     | Where the leaseholders own the freehold together, usually through a company.                                                                                     |                                                                      | Yes     |
| The Property Institute (TPI)          | The professional body for residential managing agents. It was formed from ARMA and IRPM. BPM is a Full Member.                                                   | ARMA, IRPM (except to say TPI replaced them)                         | Yes     |
| The Property Ombudsman (TPO)          | An independent service that settles complaints about property firms that are members. BPM is a member.                                                           | the ombudsman (on first use)                                         | Yes     |

## Buildings and places

| Term                 | Definition                                                                                                                   | Avoid                                                                                                      | Explain |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| block                | A building divided into flats.                                                                                               | unit (for the building), scheme                                                                            | No      |
| estate               | A group of homes that share roads, paths, green spaces or other areas that need managing.                                    | estate area (except "freehold estate area"), site                                                          | No      |
| development          | A general word for a block, an estate, or both. Use it to talk about BPM's portfolio.                                        | property (when "development" is meant)                                                                     | No      |
| flat                 | A home within a block.                                                                                                       | apartment (except in a development's own name), unit (except in counts, for example "a block of 12 units") | No      |
| communal areas       | The shared parts of a building or estate, such as halls, stairs, lifts, roofs, gardens and car parks.                        | common parts (except when quoting a lease), shared spaces                                                  | No      |
| higher-risk building | A building at least 18 metres or 7 storeys tall with at least 2 homes. Extra rules apply under the Building Safety Act 2022. | high-rise (in legal contexts)                                                                              | Yes     |

## Money

| Term                    | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Avoid                                                                                           | Explain |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| service charge          | The money leaseholders pay, under their lease, towards the cost of running, repairing and insuring the building.                                                                                                          | maintenance charge, maintenance fee, management charge                                          | Yes     |
| estate charge           | The money owners of freehold homes on an estate pay towards the shared areas.                                                                                                                                             | service charge (for freehold homes), rentcharge (except in legal detail)                        | Yes     |
| service charge budget   | The plan for the year's spending, which sets how much each leaseholder pays.                                                                                                                                              | estimate, forecast (for the budget; "estimates" is correct for contractor prices in Section 20) | No      |
| reserve fund            | Money collected and saved for large future costs, such as a new roof or redecoration.                                                                                                                                     | sinking fund (mention once as another name)                                                     | Yes     |
| service charge accounts | The year end statement of what was actually spent, compared with the budget.                                                                                                                                              | year end accounting, end-of-year accounts                                                       | No      |
| balancing charge        | The extra amount a leaseholder pays if the year's spending was more than the budget. If spending was less, the leaseholder gets a balancing credit.                                                                       | shortfall, top-up                                                                               | Yes     |
| ground rent             | A yearly payment a leaseholder may owe the freeholder under the lease. Most new long leases from 30 June 2022 can only have a peppercorn (zero) ground rent.                                                              | rent (on its own)                                                                               | Yes     |
| management fee          | What the managing agent charges for its work. It is one part of the service charge.                                                                                                                                       | agent's commission                                                                              | No      |
| administration charge   | A fee charged to one leaseholder under their lease, not to everyone. It can be for a request (such as a consent), for paying late, or for breaking a lease covenant. Whether a sale pack fee counts depends on the lease. | admin fee, admin charge                                                                         | Yes     |
| arrears                 | Service charge or ground rent that is overdue.                                                                                                                                                                            | debt (in leaseholder-facing copy)                                                               | No      |
| trust account           | A bank account for service charge money, which the law says is held on trust for the leaseholders. BPM keeps a designated trust account for each block or estate, separate from its own money.                            | client account (on its own), BPM's account                                                      | Yes     |

## Works and compliance

| Term                              | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                 | Avoid                                   | Explain |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ------- |
| repairs and maintenance           | Day-to-day work to keep a building in good order, such as fixing lights, doors or leaks.                                                                                                   | upkeep, minor works                     | No      |
| major works                       | Large, one-off projects such as roof replacement, external redecoration or lift renewal.                                                                                                   | capital works, big works                | No      |
| Section 20 consultation           | The legal process for consulting leaseholders before major works that will cost any one leaseholder more than £250. It comes from Section 20 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985.          | S20, section twenty                     | Yes     |
| long-term agreement               | A contract for services that lasts more than 12 months. If it will cost any one leaseholder more than £100 a year, Section 20 consultation applies.                                        | QLTA (except in legal detail)           | Yes     |
| site inspection                   | A regular visit by the property manager to check the condition of the building and communal areas.                                                                                         | site visit, walk-round                  | No      |
| fire risk assessment              | A written check of fire risks in the communal areas, the structure, the external walls and the flat entrance doors, with actions to reduce them. The law requires one for blocks of flats. | FRA (on first use), fire check          | Yes     |
| health and safety risk assessment | A written check of risks to people using the communal areas.                                                                                                                               | H&S assessment                          | No      |
| asbestos survey                   | A check for asbestos in the communal areas of older buildings, so it can be managed safely.                                                                                                |                                         | No      |
| Building Safety Act 2022          | The law that brought in extra safety duties for buildings, with the strictest rules for higher-risk buildings.                                                                             | BSA                                     | Yes     |
| CDM                               | The Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015. They set health and safety duties for building work.                                                                            |                                         | Yes     |
| contractor                        | A firm that carries out work on the building.                                                                                                                                              | supplier (for building work), tradesman | No      |

## Leases and legal

| Term                                            | Definition                                                                                                                            | Avoid                                                                                   | Explain |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| lease                                           | The legal agreement that gives a leaseholder ownership of a flat for a set number of years and sets out everyone's rights and duties. | contract, tenancy agreement                                                             | No      |
| lease covenants                                 | The promises in a lease, such as paying the service charge or not causing a nuisance.                                                 | rules, terms (on their own)                                                             | Yes     |
| consent                                         | Permission from the freeholder or RMC under the lease, for example to alter a flat or keep a pet.                                     | licence (except for "licence to alter" and "licence to assign")                         | Yes     |
| leasehold sale pack                             | The information a seller's solicitor needs from the managing agent when a flat is sold. It is often given on the LPE1 form.           | LPE1 pack, property sales legal support, management pack (mention once as another name) | Yes     |
| annual general meeting (AGM)                    | A yearly meeting of an RMC's or RTM company's members to review the year and make decisions.                                          | AGM (on first use)                                                                      | Yes     |
| Companies House                                 | The government body that keeps records of UK companies.                                                                               |                                                                                         | No      |
| RICS Service Charge Residential Management Code | The industry code of practice for managing residential service charges. Do not claim BPM follows it until `facts.md` supports it.     | RICS code (on first use)                                                                | Yes     |

# Messaging

What the site says about BPM, and the proof behind each claim. Built from `competitors.md`, `audiences.md` and `facts.md`. Every claim here must trace to a fact in `facts.md`.

## Positioning

**For** RMC and RTM directors, freeholders and developers with blocks of flats or shared estates in Hampshire and the south of England, **BPM is** an independent managing agent that does block and estate management and nothing else. **Unlike** national firms, clients deal with a named property manager in a Winchester office, not a call centre. **Unlike** most local firms, BPM has been doing this since 1989, and manages more than 150 developments.

## Value proposition

Independent block and estate management from Winchester since 1989, with a named property manager for every building.

## Strapline

**Block and estate management since 1989.**

On the home page, the strapline is the eyebrow line above the H1. "Block and estate management" is the term clients search for and the term competitors use. It also makes clear that BPM does not do sales or lettings. Do not use "Property management experts since 1989".

The LinkedIn tagline "Where Working Together Matters" is not used on the site. It is generic and says nothing about what BPM does.

## Key messages

### 1. Block and estate management is all we do

BPM manages residential blocks and estates. It does not sell or let property. It has done this work since 1989 and manages more than 150 developments, from blocks of 4 flats to a 22-floor high rise.

- **Proof:** established 1989; 150+ developments; range of 4 flats to 22 floors; no estate agency or lettings.
- **Beats:** younger local firms (GH 2008, HMS 2005, Adur 2011, Evolve 2016) and estate agents with a block department.

### 2. A named property manager who knows your building

Each client has one dedicated property manager, available Monday to Friday in office hours. The manager inspects the building regularly. Larger developments also get regular management reports.

- **Proof:** dedicated property manager; regular site inspections; management reports for larger developments; named team with join dates (some since 2011 and 2014).
- **Beats:** national firms with central contact teams.

### 3. Your money, handled properly

Service charge money is held in a designated trust account for each block or estate. BPM prepares budgets, plans reserve funds, collects charges, chases arrears and produces service charge accounts with its own accounts team.

- **Proof:** designated trust accounts; reserve fund management; in-house bookkeeping and credit control; £5 million professional indemnity insurance.
- **Beats:** firms that say nothing about how they hold client money.

### 4. We carry the legal weight for volunteer directors

RMC and RTM directors give their time for free but carry legal duties. BPM acts as company secretary, arranges the fire, asbestos and health and safety assessments the law requires, runs Section 20 consultations, and enforces the lease covenants. The directors keep their legal duties; BPM does the work that helps them meet them.

- **Proof:** company secretary for about 15 RMCs today (38 appointments in total, Companies House); formal fire, asbestos and health and safety assessment surveys with specialist contractors; reserve fund and major works planning; lease covenant enforcement.
- **Beats:** local firms with no compliance or Section 20 content.

### 5. Plain answers about service charges, Section 20 and major works

The site explains what things cost, why, and what happens next. Service pages say what is included and how the process works.

- **Proof:** the site itself (service pages with included lists, steps and FAQs).
- **Beats:** every local competitor. None explains these topics in full.

## Reputation

Public reviews of BPM are poor and mostly old. The site does not mention them, and it does not answer them with broad claims such as "excellent service". It builds trust with things a reader can check:

- named property managers, with photos when available
- clear routes to pay, report and contact
- the complaints procedure and TPO redress
- what each service includes and how it works

When the client supplies testimonials or response times, add them here and to `facts.md`.

## Claims we do not make

These have no source, or the source is wrong. Do not use them until `facts.md` supports them.

- "Award-winning", "leading", "national leader", "best"
- "Fast response", or any response time
- "No hidden fees", "transparent contracts" or "fixed fees" (the new site says these; there is no source)
- "24/7", "out of hours" or "emergency line"
- "Client money protection" or "CMP"
- "No commission" on insurance
- "Over 25 years", "over 20 years" or "over three decades". Use "since 1989"
- Any testimonial, case study or named development

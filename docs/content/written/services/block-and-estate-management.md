## intro

### intro.heading

What block and estate management covers

### intro.paragraphs

Running a block of flats or a shared estate takes time and carries legal duties. A managing agent takes that work on for you: the money, the repairs, the safety checks and the company paperwork.

Belgarum Property Management works for [resident management companies (RMCs) and right to manage (RTM) companies](/who-we-work-with/rmc-and-rtm-directors), run by the leaseholders. We also work for [freeholders](/who-we-work-with/freeholders-and-private-landlords) (the owners of the building and the land) and private landlords.

You get one dedicated property manager who knows your building. Block and estate management is all we do, and we've done it since 1989.

## included

### included.heading

What's included

### included.items

- A dedicated property manager, available Monday to Friday in office hours
- Regular site inspections, so your property manager sees problems in the communal areas
- Management reports for larger developments
- [Service charge budgets](/services/service-charge-budgets) (the yearly plan for what leaseholders pay), so each leaseholder knows what they will pay towards running the building
- Reserve fund planning, to save for large future costs such as a new roof
- Collection of service charges, with in-house credit control and independent solicitors for arrears
- Bookkeeping, with the money for each block or estate held in its own designated trust account, separate from our own money
- Repairs and maintenance through contractors from our database, with work tendered regularly so prices are compared
- Insurance policies, as part of managing the communal areas
- [Fire risk, asbestos and health and safety assessments](/services/fire-and-building-safety) by specialist firms, with the actions and their urgency reported to you
- Planning for major works, and [Section 20 consultation](/services/section-20-consultation) (the legal process for consulting leaseholders before major works)
- [Company secretary duties for RMCs](/services/company-secretarial): annual general meetings (AGMs), Companies House returns, the share register and a registered office
- Enforcement of lease covenants, the promises in each lease such as paying the service charge or not causing a nuisance

## steps

### 1. One named contact

You get a dedicated property manager. They know your building and are your main contact, Monday to Thursday, 9am to 5pm, and Friday, 9am to 4.30pm.

### 2. A service charge budget that saves ahead

We prepare the service charge budget, including what goes into the reserve fund. Then we collect the service charges and chase any arrears.

### 3. Regular inspections and repairs

Your property manager inspects the building and communal areas regularly. When something needs fixing, we arrange the work with a contractor.

### 4. Safety and legal checks

We arrange the fire risk, asbestos and health and safety assessments the building needs, using specialist firms. For RMCs, we also handle the company secretary work.

### 5. Accounts at the year end

We keep the books for each block or estate and produce the service charge accounts, which show what was spent against the budget. Larger developments also get regular management reports.

## faqs

### What does a managing agent do?

A managing agent runs a building or estate day to day for the people responsible for it. That usually means the freeholder, an RMC or an RTM company.

We set and collect the service charge, arrange repairs and safety checks, and keep the accounts. The service charge is the money leaseholders pay, under their lease, towards running, repairing and insuring the building.

### How much does block management cost?

It depends on the building: its size, its communal areas and the work it needs. Our management fee is one part of the service charge, and it is set out in the budget.

To find out what we would charge for your building, [request a management proposal](/contact?type=new-management).

### Do you manage small blocks?

Yes. We manage buildings from 4 flats up to a 22-floor high rise. We also work for private landlords.

### Do you manage freehold estates as well as blocks of flats?

Yes. We manage leasehold blocks and freehold estate areas. On an estate, the homeowners usually pay an estate charge towards the shared roads, paths and green spaces.

### Which areas do you cover?

Hampshire, Sussex, Dorset, London and the Home Counties. Our office is in Winchester, close to the M3.

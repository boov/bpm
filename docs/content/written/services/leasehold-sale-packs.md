## intro

### intro.heading

What a leasehold sale pack is

### intro.paragraphs

When a leaseholder sells a flat, the buyer's solicitor needs information about the building and the service charge. The managing agent provides it in a leasehold sale pack (sometimes called a management pack), often on a standard form called the LPE1. A complete pack helps the sale move without delays.

If Belgarum Property Management manages the building, we prepare the pack. We also answer the further questions the buyer's solicitor may raise. Solicitors can send enquiries to solicitorenquiries@bpmanagement.org.

## included

### included.heading

What a pack usually covers

### included.items

- The service charge for the flat, the current service charge budget and recent service charge accounts
- The reserve fund, which is money saved for large future costs
- Any arrears on the flat
- Planned major works, and any Section 20 consultation in progress
- Buildings insurance details
- Contact details for the freeholder (the owner of the building and the land), resident management company (RMC) or right to manage (RTM) company
- Building safety information, where it applies to the building

## steps

### 1. The seller's solicitor orders the pack

The seller's solicitor orders the pack from us. `[NEEDS: sale pack ordering (Q5)]` Send enquiries to solicitorenquiries@bpmanagement.org.

### 2. The seller pays the fee

The seller usually pays the pack fee. Depending on the lease, it may count as an administration charge: a fee charged to one leaseholder, not to everyone. `[NEEDS: sale pack cost (Q5)]`

### 3. We prepare the pack

We prepare the pack from the building's records, including the budgets, accounts and Section 20 records. `[NEEDS: sale pack turnaround (Q5)]`

### 4. Further questions

The buyer's solicitor may have more questions after reading the pack. Send them to solicitorenquiries@bpmanagement.org.

### 5. After the sale

After completion, the buyer's solicitor usually sends us notice of the sale. The lease may also need a deed of covenant, where the buyer promises to keep the lease covenants. A fee may apply, depending on the lease. We then update our records, so future service charge demands go to the new owner.

## proof

- The figures in the pack come from the books our own accounts team keeps for each block or estate.
- Our team has knowledge of property law, land registration and conveyancing.
- As managing agent, we hold the building's service charge budgets and Section 20 records.

## faqs

### Who orders a leasehold sale pack?

Usually the seller's solicitor. If you're selling a flat in a building we manage, tell your solicitor that we're the managing agent. Give them our address for solicitor enquiries: solicitorenquiries@bpmanagement.org.

### How much does a pack cost, and how long does it take?

`[NEEDS: sale pack cost (Q5)]` The seller usually pays the fee. Depending on the lease, it may count as an administration charge.

`[NEEDS: sale pack turnaround (Q5)]` To avoid delays, the seller's solicitor should order the pack as soon as a sale is agreed.

### What is the LPE1 form?

The LPE1 is a standard form for leasehold property enquiries, published by the Law Society. It sets out the questions a buyer's solicitor usually asks about the building, the service charge and the lease.

### I am selling a freehold house on an estate you manage. Do I need a pack?

Usually, yes. The buyer's solicitor will want details of the estate charge, which is the money homeowners pay towards the shared areas of the estate. This information is often given on a standard form called the FME1.

### I am selling my flat. What can I do to help the sale go smoothly?

Make sure your service charge is paid up to date, because any arrears show in the pack. Tell your solicitor early that we manage your building. If you have questions about your service charge, contact your property manager.

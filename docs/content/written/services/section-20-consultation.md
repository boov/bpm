## intro

### intro.heading

What Section 20 consultation is

### intro.paragraphs

If major works will cost any one leaseholder more than £250, the law says leaseholders must be consulted first. This is called Section 20 consultation. It comes from Section 20 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985.

The duty sits with whoever manages the building under the leases. That can be the freeholder (the owner of the building and the land), a resident management company (RMC) or a right to manage (RTM) company. Belgarum Property Management runs the process for them, from the first notice to the choice of contractor.

What's included

## steps

### 1. Notice of intention

We send every leaseholder a notice that describes the works and why they are needed. Leaseholders have 30 days to comment and to nominate a contractor.

### 2. Estimates

We get estimates from contractors, including any nominated contractor where the law requires it. At least one estimate must come from a contractor with no connection to the freeholder, RMC or RTM company.

### 3. Statement of estimates

We send leaseholders a statement of at least 2 estimates, with a summary of the comments from stage 1. All the estimates are available for leaseholders to see. Leaseholders have another 30 days to comment.

### 4. Choosing the contractor

The freeholder or board chooses the contractor, taking the comments into account. If they do not choose the lowest estimate or a nominated contractor, we send a notice of reasons within 21 days of signing the contract.

### 5. The works

Once the contractor is appointed, the works can start. Read about how we plan and run [major works](/services/major-works). Planning works for your board or building? [Request a management proposal](/contact?type=new-management).

## faqs

### What happens if Section 20 consultation is not done properly?

If the freeholder, RMC or RTM company does not consult properly, they can usually recover only £250 from each leaseholder for the works. The rest must be paid from elsewhere, unless a tribunal agrees to set the requirements aside. This is called dispensation. So every step and every deadline matters.

### Does Section 20 consultation mean leaseholders can stop the works?

No. Leaseholders have a right to be told about the works, to comment and to nominate a contractor. The freeholder or board must take the comments into account, but they make the final decision.

If you think the cost is unreasonable, you can take advice about your own case.

### How long does Section 20 consultation take?

At least 60 days. The process has two 30-day periods for leaseholders to comment, and getting estimates between them adds more time. Plan for this before the works are due.

### Does Section 20 apply to contracts as well as works?

Yes. A long-term agreement is a contract for services that lasts more than 12 months, such as a cleaning contract. If it will cost any one leaseholder more than £100 a year, it needs Section 20 consultation too.

### I have received a Section 20 notice. What should I do?

Read it, and note the date the consultation period ends. If you want to comment or nominate a contractor, do it in writing before that date. If you have questions about the notice, contact your property manager.

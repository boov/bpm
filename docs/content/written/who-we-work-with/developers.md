### intro.paragraphs

How a development will be managed affects its running costs, its service charge and the questions buyers' solicitors ask. The best time to plan it is before the first home is sold.

Belgarum Property Management advises developers to involve the managing agent (the firm that runs the development day to day) from the planning stage. We've managed blocks of flats and shared estates since 1989. Today we manage over 150 developments, from blocks of 4 flats to a 22-floor high rise.

## faqs

### When should a developer involve a managing agent?

At the planning stage. Decisions made early, such as the design of the shared areas, affect what the development costs to run for years to come.

### What is the difference between a service charge and an estate charge?

A [service charge](/services/service-charge-budgets) is what leaseholders pay, under their lease, towards running, repairing and insuring a block of flats. An estate charge is what owners of freehold homes pay towards the shared areas of an estate, such as roads, paths and green spaces.

### Do new leases have ground rent?

Ground rent is a yearly payment a leaseholder may owe the freeholder under the lease. The Leasehold Reform (Ground Rent) Act 2022 applies to most new long residential leases granted from 30 June 2022. These leases can only have a peppercorn (zero) ground rent. There are exceptions, including rent on the landlord's share of a shared ownership lease.

### What fire and building safety rules apply to a new block of flats?

Every block of flats needs a fire risk assessment of its communal areas, structure, external walls and flat entrance doors. Buildings at least 18 metres or 7 storeys tall, with at least 2 homes, are higher-risk buildings. Under the Building Safety Act 2022, they must be registered with the Building Safety Regulator before residents move in, and extra rules apply.
